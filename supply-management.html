The Challenge

Why Growing Businesses Struggle to Manage Supply Chains Consistently

As order volumes grow, managing an international supply chain becomes a full-time job — one that most South African businesses are not resourced to handle internally.

Inconsistent Supplier Performance
Suppliers that perform well on initial orders begin to slip on quality or lead times once the relationship is taken for granted — causing stock shortages and quality failures.
No Leverage for Price Renegotiation
Without ongoing market intelligence and supplier competition, buyers pay more than they need to as volumes grow — margin that should be captured stays with the factory.
Stock-Outs and Overstock Cycles
Without proactive supply planning, businesses cycle between running out of stock and over-ordering to compensate — tying up cash and creating operational chaos.
Quality Creep Over Time
Quality standards degrade gradually across repeat orders without active oversight — often going unnoticed until customer complaints or retail returns spike.
Internal Resource Drain
Managing supplier communication, order tracking, quality follow-ups, and logistics across multiple suppliers consumes internal team time that should be spent on growing the business.
Single-Supplier Dependency Risk
Businesses that rely on a single overseas supplier for a key product are highly exposed to disruption — with no backup plan if the relationship fails or the supplier cannot deliver.
Our Solution

Your Dedicated Supply Chain Team, Without the Overhead

We embed into your business as an ongoing supply partner — managing your supplier relationships, repeat orders, quality oversight, logistics, and strategic supply planning so you can focus on growth.

  • 1
    Supplier Relationship Management
    We maintain active relationships with your suppliers — keeping them accountable on pricing, lead times, quality, and responsiveness on your behalf.
  • 2
    Order Planning and Placement
    We manage your reorder cycle — tracking stock levels, advising reorder points, placing purchase orders, and coordinating with suppliers to maintain availability.
  • 3
    Ongoing Quality Oversight
    We conduct regular quality inspections on repeat orders — catching standard drift before it becomes a problem and maintaining consistent product quality.
  • 4
    Logistics and Clearance Management
    We manage freight, customs clearance, and delivery coordination for every shipment — consistently and without you having to manage it order by order.
  • 5
    Strategic Supply Reviews
    We conduct regular supply chain reviews — identifying cost savings, backup supplier opportunities, and strategic improvements to keep your supply chain optimised as your business grows.
How It Works

How We Manage Your Supply Chain Ongoing

From onboarding your suppliers to managing every order — a structured partnership that keeps your supply chain running smoothly.

01

Supply Chain Audit

We review your current supplier relationships, order history, pain points, and supply requirements to establish a clear baseline.

02

Supplier Onboarding

We establish direct working relationships with your approved suppliers — aligning on quality standards, lead times, communication, and commercial terms.

03

Order Planning Setup

We set up your reorder schedule and stock planning framework — so orders are placed proactively, not reactively, to keep shelves stocked.

04

Ongoing Order Management

We manage every order from placement to delivery — purchase orders, production follow-up, inspections, freight, and customs on your behalf.

05

Reporting & Visibility

You receive regular updates and reporting on order status, quality outcomes, lead times, and landed costs — with full visibility at all times.

06

Strategic Reviews

We meet regularly to review performance, identify improvements, renegotiate pricing, and plan ahead for seasonal demand or business growth.
