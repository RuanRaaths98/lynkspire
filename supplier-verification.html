Supplier Verification

Know Exactly Who You Are Buying From Before You Send Any Money

We conduct thorough verification of overseas suppliers — confirming legitimacy, manufacturing capability, financial standing, and trade record — so you never have to take a supplier at face value.

Request Verification → View All Services
The Challenge

Why Trusting Unverified Suppliers Is a Costly Risk

Online marketplaces and trade directories make it easy to find suppliers — but they offer no guarantee of legitimacy, capability, or reliability.

Trading Companies Posing as Factories
Many online "manufacturers" are actually middlemen who add margin and have limited control over quality, lead times, or production issues.
Fraudulent Suppliers and Scams
International B2B fraud is widespread. Buyers have lost significant sums to suppliers who disappear after receiving deposits or down payments.
Inflated Credentials and Certifications
Suppliers regularly present certificates and audit reports that are outdated, forged, or obtained from unreliable third parties — with no way to verify them remotely.
No On-the-Ground Verification
Without the ability to physically visit a factory or conduct local due diligence, buyers have no way to confirm what they're told is actually true.
Capacity Misrepresentation
Factories overstate production capacity to win orders, then subcontract to unvetted facilities — resulting in quality and compliance failures you weren't prepared for.
Financial Exposure on Unverified Orders
Paying deposits or full orders to unverified suppliers exposes your business to significant financial loss with limited legal recourse across jurisdictions.
Our Solution

Independent Supplier Due Diligence You Can Trust

We conduct structured verification using a combination of document review, trade record checks, and in-country contacts — giving you a clear, independent picture of who you are dealing with before any commitment is made.

  • 1
    Business Registration & Legal Status
    We confirm the supplier is a legally registered business in their country, with valid trading status and the correct business category.
  • 2
    Factory vs. Trading Company Confirmation
    We establish whether you are dealing directly with a manufacturer or a trading intermediary — and what that means for your pricing and control.
  • 3
    Certification & Compliance Verification
    We verify the authenticity and current validity of certifications, audit reports, and quality management credentials presented by the supplier.
  • 4
    Trade Record & Reference Checks
    We review the supplier's export history, trade volume, and where possible obtain references from existing buyers in their network.
  • 5
    Verification Report & Recommendation
    We deliver a structured written report with our findings and a clear recommendation — proceed, proceed with conditions, or avoid.
How It Works

The Verification Process, Step by Step

A structured, independent assessment of your supplier before any money changes hands.

01

Submit Supplier Details

Share the supplier's name, location, website, and any documentation they have provided. We respond within 24 hours.

02

Document Review

We review all supplier-provided documents including business licences, certificates, and company registrations.

03

Independent Cross-Check

We independently verify claims against government registries, trade databases, and certification bodies.

04

Factory / Company Assessment

Where required, we conduct on-the-ground or agent-based assessments to confirm physical existence and capability.

05

Trade Record Check

We review export history, trade volume, and buyer references where accessible to assess actual track record.

06

Written Report & Recommendation

You receive a clear, structured verification report with findings and our independent recommendation.
