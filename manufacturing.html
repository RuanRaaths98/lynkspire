The Challenge

Why Custom Manufacturing Is Difficult Without the Right Partner

Building a product from scratch through an overseas factory is one of the most complex supply chain undertakings for any South African business.

No Factory Access or Tooling Knowledge
Getting a product manufactured requires tooling, moulds, and prototyping — processes that most buyers have no experience navigating with overseas factories.
Counterfeit and Substandard Output
Without oversight during production, factories substitute materials or cut corners — resulting in products that look right in samples but fail at scale.
Missed Deadlines and Long Lead Times
Without active production management, factories deprioritise smaller buyers and delivery timelines slip — killing product launch plans and retailer commitments.
Unclear Pricing and Hidden Costs
OEM and private label quotes from factories often exclude tooling, packaging, compliance, and freight — making accurate landed cost calculations near impossible.
Branding and Compliance Requirements
Getting factories to apply your branding, labelling, and meet South African compliance requirements requires clear technical briefs and ongoing oversight.
No Visibility During Production
Once a purchase order is placed, buyers have no insight into production status until the goods are ready to ship — leaving no time to address issues.
Our Solution

Full Manufacturing Management from Brief to Branded Product

We act as your manufacturing partner on the ground — managing factory selection, tooling, production scheduling, quality, and compliance so your product is built to specification, on time.

How It Works

The Manufacturing Process, Step by Step

From your product concept to branded goods delivered in South Africa — a structured, managed process at every stage.

01

Product Brief

Share your product concept, materials, quantities, branding, and target cost. We respond within 24 hours.

02

Factory Selection

We identify and shortlist OEM factories with the right capability, certifications, and track record for your product.

03

Quotation & Tooling

We gather fully-costed quotes including tooling, moulds, packaging, and compliance requirements.

04

Sampling & Approval

05

Production & QC

We manage production milestones and conduct quality checks throughout — with pre-shipment inspection before release.

06

Shipping & Delivery
